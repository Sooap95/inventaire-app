This distribution includes the unmodified browser release of html5-qrcode,
including its bundled ZXing barcode decoder (https://github.com/zxing-js/library).
These projects are licensed under Apache License 2.0. The upstream license is
included as LICENSE-html5-qrcode.txt. Copyrights remain with their contributors.

No external OCR, product lookup, telemetry or media upload is added by this app.

Enhanced local decoder: zxing-wasm 2.2.1, reader-only distribution.
https://github.com/Sec-ant/zxing-wasm/tree/v2.2.1
Wrapper: MIT (LICENSE-zxing-wasm.txt); ZXing-C++ and C++ wrapper: Apache-2.0
(LICENSE-zxing-cpp.txt, LICENSE-zxing-wrapper.txt).
ZXing-C++ commit: c0a7c6cd2165457c721c58045edd22ffee9624e9.
The reader JS and WASM files are unmodified upstream artifacts.
No writer/zint artifact is included. All assets are served on this site only.
